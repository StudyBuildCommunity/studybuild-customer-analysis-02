## 📌 Project Overview

### Scenario

As a Junior Data Analyst at an e-commerce company, I was tasked with analyzing customer data to answer five key business questions:

1. Which customers are most valuable to the company?
2. Which customers are at risk of churning?
3. Which city should be targeted for the next marketing campaign?
4. Are discounts actually effective at increasing sales?
5. What actions should be taken to increase revenue?

### Approach

Using **Python** and libraries such as `pandas`, `numpy`, and `matplotlib`, I performed:
- **RFM-style analysis** to identify high-value customers
- **Churn prediction** based on recency thresholds
- **City performance scoring** using multiple business metrics
- **Discount impact analysis** comparing customer groups
- **Customer segmentation** into Champions, Loyal, At Risk, and Needs Attention
- **Pareto analysis** to check revenue concentration (80/20 rule)
- **Executive dashboard** with 3 key visualizations for the CEO
